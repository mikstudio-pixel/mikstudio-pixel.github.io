Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Barcode Alarm app handles information when you use the Android mobile application.

Last updated: March 23, 2026 App version: 0.1.0
Contact details updated: this page is ready to publish for your GitHub Pages privacy policy.

1. Who We Are

Barcode Alarm is provided by the developer of the application. The app is designed for Android devices and is used to set alarms, snooze them, and dismiss them by scanning a barcode.

2. What Data the App Processes

The app may process the following categories of data:

Based on the current implementation, this data is primarily processed and stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer's own server.

  • Alarm settings, such as time, repeat schedule, alarm label, and enabled status.
  • Saved barcode data, such as decoded value, barcode format, and user-defined label.
  • Optional location data, if the user enables the safe dismissal feature outside a selected radius.
  • Technical information related to purchasing the Pro version through Google Play.
  • Local app state, such as available "hearts" for snoozing and related preferences.

3. How the App Uses Permissions

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes needed to set up or dismiss an alarm. Based on the current implementation, captured images are not sent to the developer's own server.

Location

Location is optional and is used only for the feature that checks whether the user is outside a previously selected radius. If you do not use this feature, location is not processed.

Notifications and Alarms

Permissions for notifications, exact alarms, vibration, and background services are used only to ensure that alarms ring reliably and can appear on the lock screen.

Biometrics

If the app offers biometric authentication, it uses the Android system interface. The app does not store fingerprints or other biometric templates.

4. Where Data Is Stored

Based on the current implementation, the app's main data is stored locally on the user's device, especially in the app's internal database and local Android settings.

If the user has Android system backup enabled on their device, some locally stored data may be included in cloud backup or device transfer according to the operating system rules.

5. Sharing Data with Third Parties

Based on the currently available code, the app does not use its own remote server to store user data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not include separate third-party analytics or crash reporting.

The app is designed so that alarm settings, barcode content, and optional location-related settings remain on the user's device unless Android system services or Google Play services process limited technical data required for their functionality.

However, some features rely on Google or Android system services and libraries, including:

  • Google Play Billing for processing purchases of the Pro version.
  • Google ML Kit for on-device barcode recognition.
  • Android system services for notifications, biometric authentication, alarms, and possible backups.

These services are governed by the terms and privacy policies of their respective providers.

6. How We Use Data

  • To provide the main functionality of the app, including setting, triggering, and dismissing alarms.
  • To match alarms with barcodes saved by the user.
  • To optionally verify location for safe alarm dismissal.
  • To unlock and manage Pro features purchased through Google Play.
  • To store local preferences and restore app behavior after device restart.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as it remains stored on the user's device, or until the user deletes it by removing specific items, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.

8. Data Sales and Advertising

We do not sell users' personal data. Based on the current implementation, the app does not display third-party advertising.

9. Children

The app is not designed as a service exclusively for children. If you believe the app processes a child's data in violation of applicable law, please contact us using the details below.

10. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may deny optional permissions, such as location or camera access, if you do not want to use those features.
  • You may remove locally stored data by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.
  • You may manage system backup and permissions directly in your device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, especially if the app's features, used services, or legal requirements change. The current version will always be published on this page.
